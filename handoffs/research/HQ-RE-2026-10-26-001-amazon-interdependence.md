It is a water **flux**, not 20 billion tonnes accumulating daily in the atmosphere. |
| Amazon moisture affects people elsewhere; a single clearing event directly causes a specific distant disaster. | [Zemp et al., *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics* 2014](https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/14/13337/2014/acp-14-13337-2014.html) use historical data and a moisture-tracking model: cascading land recycling accounts for ~9–10% of South American precipitation, ~17–18% in La Plata basin; Amazon-origin share there in wet season rises from 18–23% to 24–29% when cascades counted. [Cui et al., *Nature Communications* 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-68361-z) combine observations and a moisture model for 1980–2019, finding southern basin rainfall fell ~8–11%; they attribute ~52–72% of that decline to southern/upwind forest loss, with model and extrapolation uncertainty. | **Regional transboundary dependency well supported; particular downstream outcomes probabilistic.** The La Plata percentages are modeled, season-specific and include recycled moisture; they are not a fraction of all global rainfall. Claims about a particular flood, person, country outside South America or individual's purchase need separate evidence. Global climate relevance is credible through carbon feedback, but precise remote rainfall teleconnections are much less settled. |
| The Amazon is the world's “lungs” supplying a large net share of our oxygen. | [Oxford Nature Network / Yadvinder Malhi, 2021](https://nature.ox.ac.uk/research_stories/rainforests-are-not-the-lungs-of-our-planet/) explains that photosynthetic oxygen is largely used in plant respiration, decomposition and fire; land ecosystem **net** oxygen production is close to zero. [NOAA ocean-oxygen explanation](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/ocean-oxygen.html) similarly distinguishes gross production from consumption and the ancient atmospheric reservoir. | **Misleading oxygen metaphor.** Forest protection matters for carbon, water, biodiversity and people; it will not avert an imminent lack of breathable oxygen. |

The Gatti carbon-stock estimate spans Amazon vegetation and soil; Flores percentages concern an original transnational forest biome; INPE's annual PRODES series concerns the Brazilian Legal Amazon. Their values answer different questions. None is a direct measure of the others.

## What the source material would overstate

Treat as unsupported or misleading any claim that the 25 October vote by itself fixes the forest's fate; that the present annual INPE decline proves no further risk, or a cumulative 20–25% figure proves imminent collapse; that 20 billion tonnes/day is a fresh observed measurement; that one can add degraded and deforested shares; that the forest supplies a fifth of **net** planetary oxygen; or that one phone purchase, vote, or individual outside Brazil can be identified as the cause of a particular fire, drought or loss. The report does not authenticate wording in the supplied post or grant reuse of its image.

## Defensible explanation and PSI relevance

Trees draw water from soil and release vapour through leaves. Winds carry some of it across the forest and farther downwind; some rains, evaporates and rains again. Forest change can therefore alter water supply and rainfall conditions beyond the cleared location. The best quantified paths here cross **South America**. Carbon stored in forest and soil also matters to the global climate; its release and regrowth are variable, gradual and disturbance-dependent. This supports ecological interdependence without proving a specific person's harm or moral duty.

**Owner learning — a threshold is not a stopwatch.** A model may show that beyond a specified amount of clearing, feedback between fewer trees, less local rain and more fire accelerates loss. The result depends on the model's climate, geography and disturbance assumptions. It does not mean a satellite has detected one universal 20% tripwire, or that an election starts a countdown. Ask “20% of which forest, measured how, under which climate and with what uncertainty?” before repeating such a number.
