## Accepted meaning and possible inquiry

The existing [AP interaction contract](https://github.com/aakas7125/PLANETARY-SELF-INQUIRY/blob/main/contracts/AP_INTERACTION_V0.md) §1 keeps Direct AP, Project Interpretation and empirical domains distinct. §§9–10 restrict new Source Lenses and permit the post-evidence Otherness question only after an evidence-backed consequence outside the immediate circle is actually shown. The frozen [AP corpus](https://github.com/aakas7125/PLANETARY-SELF-INQUIRY/blob/main/provenance/source-packages/AP-CORPUS-V0.3.0.md) requires a reviewable source snapshot and versioned approval for new Direct AP use. Constitution principles 3–7, 10–16 preserve uncertainty, non-diagnosis and user choice. Nothing here modifies APQ/QSP or makes Protect My Four an AP quotation.

The Research questions are suitable **evaluation probes**, with one correction for precision: “the people who grow food or work outdoors” may imply all such workers share the same heat loss, so the test card should ground it in *some outdoor workers under particular conditions*. The Himalayan question's “a familiar source of water or food” should let a participant answer “I do not know” or identify no relevant connection; a diagram must not assert their own water comes from those glaciers. A person may disagree with the philosophical inference while understanding the mechanism. Ask comprehension separately from agreement or persuasion.

**Recommended evaluation order:** (1) heat: one short conditional workday narrative with clear potential-versus-observed label, then ask participants to explain what is known and unknown; (2) water: a seasonal basin diagram showing glacier, rain and other water sources, then check whether participants overgeneralize “two billion” or predict a guaranteed flood/drought.
