# 1. Ready-to-use English public copy

## What PSI promises you

Planetary Self-Inquiry is built to help you look more clearly at an ordinary decision without deciding for you. It brings together your own answers, evidence and modelling, and a clearly labelled philosophical lens — then returns the choice to you.

### Your words stay yours

PSI asks questions rather than telling you what is happening “deep down.” Your raw answers remain the authority. If you are unsure or disagree, that uncertainty or disagreement can remain exactly that.

### Evidence should show its limits

PSI shows what the evidence supports, the assumptions being used, and what is still unknown. Unknown does not become zero, “no impact,” or certainty just because a number is missing.

Important claims, sources, limits, confidence and modelling boundaries should remain inspectable. Different kinds of consequences — such as climate, materials, water, waste, people and ecosystems — stay distinct rather than being collapsed into one environmental or moral score.

Evidence, modelling assumptions and philosophical interpretation stay distinguishable. A philosophical source, including Acharya Prashant, is not treated as scientific proof. “Protect My Four” is PSI’s Project Interpretation, not a direct Acharya Prashant claim.

### Consequences without blame

PSI can make wider material, human and ecological pathways understandable without telling you that one person’s phone decision caused a particular victim or event. Modelled or conditional pathways are presented as modelled or conditional, not as realized individual harm.

### Design should clarify, not steer

Simple language, visuals and engagement can help understanding, but they must not erase uncertainty or push you toward a preferred purchase outcome. PSI does not use guilt or shame as a tactic to force a “right” answer.

Where a distinction matters scientifically, simplification should not erase it. PSI also tries to ask you mainly for things you can reasonably know or estimate; technical assumptions belong in the evidence or model layer rather than in guesses demanded from you.

### Inquiry, not a verdict

PSI’s intended intervention is inquiry and clearer seeing. It does not promise enlightenment, psychological change, a smaller footprint, or any particular behaviour.

There is no hidden correct purchase and no moral score. Keep, repair, used or second-hand, refurbished, new, investigate further and unclear can all remain legitimate where applicable.

The final choice is yours. PSI does not select it for you.

### Privacy in this public beta

For this public beta, the accepted profile is anonymous: no account or login, no analytics or tracking, no non-essential cookies, and no persistent user-data store.

That is deliberately narrower than promising that every answer always stays only on your device, that data is automatically deleted, or that every possible deployment has identical privacy properties. PSI should make broader privacy claims only when the released implementation has actually been verified to support them.

### Hold us to these promises

If PSI’s wording, evidence or behaviour seems to break these commitments, you can raise it in the [public project issue tracker](https://github.com/aakas7125/PLANETARY-SELF-INQUIRY/issues/new).

GitHub issues are public. Please do not post private answers, sensitive information or personal details there.

Feedback can trigger investigation, but it does not automatically become scientific, philosophical or product authority.

If visual polish, engagement or completion pressure conflicts with these commitments, these commitments come first.

# 3. Six-scene static narrative/content map

The visual sequence is explanatory only. It must not show a virtuous winner, a guilty user, a harmed named person, or a deterministic causal line from one purchase to one victim/event.

| Scene | Content IDs | Static visual intent | What it communicates | Misleading implication to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Your life is where the question starts | PC-OPEN-01, PC-RAW-01 | An ordinary desk/home/life scene: a phone among other everyday objects; a person and a small circle of people they care about. | PSI starts from a person’s actual life and their own words, not from a predetermined moral verdict. | Do not imply “your four” are the only people who matter, or that close relationships prove a particular choice. |
| 2. Ordinary choices repeat | PC-CHOICE-01, PC-INPUT-01 | Several ordinary choice paths shown side-by-side: keep, repair, used, refurbished, new, investigate. None highlighted as morally superior. | Everyday decisions accumulate and deserve examination; options remain open. | No green/red moral coding, trophy, halo, guilt icon, or default “correct” route. |
| 3. Choices sit inside wider systems | PC-CAUSE-01, PC-PMF-01 | A wide systems diagram: materials, processing, components, transport, use/repair, second life, end-of-life, people and ecosystems. | Phone choices connect to wider systems and supported aggregate pathways. | No arrow from “your phone” to a named victim, dead animal, flooded home or specific worker. No claim that one phone caused a realized event. |
| 4. Evidence has edges | PC-EVID-01, PC-EVID-02, PC-EVID-03, PC-SEPARATE-01, PC-DISTINCT-01 | Source cards, ranges, “unknown” markers, conditional/modelled labels, separate domain columns. | PSI shows evidence with sources, assumptions, uncertainty and distinctions. | No single green score, certainty gauge, fake precision, or visual blending of AP philosophy into scientific evidence. |
| 5. Questions, not judgement | PC-RAW-01, PC-DESIGN-01, PC-DESIGN-02, PC-INQUIRY-01 | A notebook/reflection surface preserving literal user answers, including “I’m not sure”; no psychological labels. | PSI asks; it does not diagnose hidden motives, score virtue or use shame to force an outcome. | Do not visually depict disagreement as denial, uncertainty as failure, or a purchase desire as greed/ego. |
| 6. The choice returns to you | PC-CHOICE-02, PC-PRIV-01, PC-PRIV-02, PC-ACCOUNT-01, PC-ACCOUNT-02, PC-ACCOUNT-03, PC-CONFLICT-01 | Open choice panel with no preselection, a small privacy note, and a visible “Hold us to this” public feedback link. | The user retains the final choice; PSI remains accountable to its own rules. | No celebratory “good” ending tied to one purchase; no private-contact implication; no device-only/deletion guarantee. |

### Static-first rule

The six scenes can be implemented as illustrated static sections, diagrams or composed cards. Animation is optional later and must satisfy Constitution P14. Motion is not necessary for semantic completeness.
