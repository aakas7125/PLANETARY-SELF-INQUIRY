## Visual language observed

- Palette: warm cream ground, dark forest-green text, coral/red section chips,
  plum and lavender accent shapes. The environmental-impact sequence switches
  to charcoal with orange/red globe imagery.
- Typography: a large bold condensed serif/sans display face paired with a
  cursive accent face; body text is a rounded sans.
- Layout: large section title; a single illustrated phone or globe; short
  grouped text blocks; generous empty space; simple circular icon diagrams.
- Motion: text and diagrams reveal progressively. Use the listed later frames
  for complete information; early same-slide frames may be mid-animation.

## Data transcribed from the most legible frames

These are source-video claims, copied for visual-reference work only:

- Raw-material composition: “40 percent metals, 40 percent plastics, and 20
  percent ceramics and trace materials.”
- Integrated-circuit production: “2.7–4.3 kgCO2e per cm2” for the stated IC
  production/life-cycle context; average GWP stated as “about 3.5 kg CO2e per
  cm2.”
- Display production: electricity consumption stated as “about 0.1 kWh/cm2
  (including the touch layer).”
- Use stage: representative user changes phone every 3 years; light/heavy-user
  operating lives are stated as 4 and 2 years respectively.
- Environmental-impact summary: manufacturing stated as 85–95% of the phone’s
  entire carbon footprint; usage slide states an average of 63 kg CO2 from one
  hour of smartphone use per day.

Do not treat these numbers as canonical project values without an evidence and
provenance review against the original cited material.

## Recommended use in a vision pass

Use the life-cycle overview and the end-of-life diagram as compositional
references. Treat the dense source slides as evidence of the desired visual
hierarchy—one claim cluster per stage—rather than copyable content. Preserve
project-specific source, uncertainty, and attribution boundaries in any new
design.
