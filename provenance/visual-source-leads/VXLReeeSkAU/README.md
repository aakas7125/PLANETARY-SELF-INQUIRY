## Audit scope and method

Two passes are preserved as original text. This section summarizes the method those files state. It does not replace their observations.

### Sampled 720p pass

Authority: [coverage-log.md](coverage-log.md) and the v1 columns of the register.

- Local 720p copy of the full 2:15:51 video.
- Sampled scan, not every frame.
- 10-second contact sheets across the video.
- A second 10-second sweep, offset by 5 seconds, for 5:05–2:15.
- 4-second sweeps for 21:02–31:52.
- Bright-region event detection at 1 fps to flag graphic overlays.
- Lower-third strip crops about every 4 seconds inside detected runs, up to about 1:56:40.
- Full-resolution frames or crops of every logged graphic.
- No audio and no transcript were used.
- 1080p was not downloaded for this pass.

### Targeted 1080p follow-up

Authority: [CHANGELOG_v2.md](CHANGELOG_v2.md). The changelog describes a follow-up, not a full rescan.

- Public 1080p (format 137) windows, no cookies, via yt-dlp section download.
- Windows: 05:10–05:50, 07:00–07:25, 09:30–09:50, 17:50–19:50, 21:05–22:15, 44:30–44:55, and 1:46:05–1:46:40.
- Frames and crops were viewed directly. Timestamps are ±2 seconds.
- No transcript, no fact-checking, and no unit conversion.
- v2 keeps the v1 text and adds four columns, plus register row 52.
- A lower-third strip (bottom 30 percent, central 76 percent of the frame) was then sampled every 2 seconds from 00:31:52 to 02:15:51 (3,120 frames), with two colour detectors. That pass added row 52 and no new source banner.

## Limitations that remain

- **Sampling gaps.** Banners shorter than the sample interval can be missed. The 2-second rescan can still miss items shorter than 2 seconds. The detectors are colour-based. Intervals before 31:52 were not part of that 2-second pass. A gap in a sampled window is not proof that a source never appeared. Uncertain intervals are listed in [coverage-log.md](coverage-log.md).
- **Unreadable text.** Exact curve values, some axis ticks, colour-key meanings, and some footers stayed unread. [unresolved-references.md](unresolved-references.md) is the 720p list. [CHANGELOG_v2.md](CHANGELOG_v2.md) and the v2 register columns record later 1080p readings for the windows that were opened. Both are kept. v2 does not overwrite v1 wording.
- **Blank spoken-claim column.** See above. There is no spoken-claim text to quote from this package.
- **Displayed text is uncorrected.** Spelling and units remain as seen on the frame. The audit did not convert units.
- **Images stay local.** Screenshot filename fields point at local images. Those images are not in this repository. Hashes are in [local-screenshot-inventory.md](local-screenshot-inventory.md).
- **Screenshot count records differ.** This inventory lists 60 `shots/` hashes and 16 `shots_v2/` hashes. The 2026-10-17 owner intake recorded an archive listing of 57 JPEG files in the v1 zip and 16 in the v2 zip. Both records are preserved. This package does not reconcile the difference.
