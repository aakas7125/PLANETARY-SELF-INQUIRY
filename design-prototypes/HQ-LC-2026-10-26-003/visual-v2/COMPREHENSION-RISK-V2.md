# Comprehension-risk assessment — Heat story visual V2

**Purpose:** Anticipate what a first-time viewer might still misunderstand, and how to test it. This is not a completed participant study.

## Likely residual risks

| Risk | Why it might still happen | Mitigation already in V2 | How to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treating **247 billion** as counted missed shifts / payroll | Large round number reads like a measured total | On-panel “modelled national potential — not payroll…” | Ask: “Is this a count of actual missed paychecks, a model estimate, or unsure?” |
| Inferring a **specific household or farm** lost income | Scene shows people at work; Path B mentions earnings pressure | Hypothetical badge; Path B uses “possible… if”; evidence qualification | Ask whether the picture documents a real family loss |
| Reading Path A as **guaranteed safety** if hours shift | Adaptation language can sound like a fix | Explicit note: early/late not guaranteed safe | Ask what could still go wrong even if hours shift |
| Reading Path B as **inevitable** harm | Branching can feel like prediction | “Neither path automatic”; “not inevitable” | Ask if Path B always happens |
| Assuming the depicted field **feeds the viewer** | Food-pathway strip can overclaim interdependence | “Illustrative pathway — not proof…” | Ask if the strip proves their food comes from that field |
| Collapsing **Lancet / AP / PSI** into one voice | Three sources in one graphic | Distinct badges + separation strip | Ask which part is science, which is AP discussion, which is PSI’s question |
| Treating the PSI question as a **scientific conclusion** or **AP quotation** | Questions next to evidence feel authoritative | Inquiry labelled PSI interpretation; “no required answer” | Ask whether the purple question is Lancet’s finding, AP’s words, or PSI’s ask |
| Over-reading **food-system** link as proven planetary guilt | Interdependence strip is easy to moralize | Conditional framing in header; open “what, if anything” | Invite “I don’t know” / disagreement; record without correcting first |

## Recommended first comprehension check (from accepted prototype)

Show **only** the static V2 visual (desktop or mobile — record which). Do not show the held video.

Without explaining, ask the participant to describe:
1. The chain from heat to work and a *possible* household or food-system effect  
2. One uncertainty or condition that changes the outcome  
3. Which part is Lancet evidence, which is AP discussion, which is PSI’s question  

Invite “I do not know” and disagreement. Record their words and any mistaken inference **without correcting** during the first response. Do not score agreement, ask about guilt, or push a preferred decision.

### Pass signal (prototype standard)
A suitable response identifies a **conditional** link and the **modelled / potential** status of the national figure.

### Revise signal
Confusion that treats the figure as observed family losses, collapses Science/AP/PSI, or treats the PSI question as a scientific finding.

## Out of scope for this artifact
No recruitment, no public copy, no website feature, no new scientific investigation. If a future test needs the exact Lancet indicator codes spoken aloud, reopen the India data sheet; V2 already uses the accepted story’s figures and definitions.
