# Heat story visual — Revision note V2

**Date:** 26 October 2026 (Europe/Berlin)  
**Status:** Offline vision-review artifact only — not public PSI copy  
**Authority:** Vision Manager handoff (retain four-stage structure; redesign Evidence & Inquiry; clarify Work; hold video)

## What changed from V1

### Kept from V1
- Four-stage sequence: **Scene → Work → Evidence → Inquiry**
- Restrained warm documentary/editorial tone
- Hypothetical / non-guilt framing
- No new scientific values, AP quotations, or PSI questions beyond the accepted prototype

### Scene (Panel 1)
- Replaced barren/drought-leaning field feel with green crop rows and ordinary agricultural surroundings under heat haze
- Distant anonymous worker silhouettes only; explicit **Hypothetical** badge
- Caption clarifies figures are not documented Lancet individuals
- Added modest interdependence strip: **Field / harvest → Food systems → Everyday food**, with an on-panel note that this is illustrative, not proof of supply to the visitor
- No disaster or suffering imagery

### Work (Panel 2)
- Removed simplified clock/sun timeline that could imply early/late work is automatically safe
- Replaced with a **conditional branch**:
  - **Path A — Can adapt:** shift hours, breaks, protections; work may still be completed
  - **Path B — Cannot adapt enough:** slow/pause; possible capacity loss; possible earnings/production pressure *if* work cannot be made up
- Visible disclaimer: neither path automatic; wider consequences not inevitable

### Evidence (Panel 3)
- **Removed decorative placeholder bar charts and invented axes** (V1 failure mode)
- Replaced with a source-grounded evidence card:
  - **247 billion** potential labour hours lost to heat exposure in 2024 (Lancet Countdown 2025 — India)
  - **66%** of those modelled potential hours in agriculture
- Explicit qualification on-panel: modelled national **potential** — not payroll, not measured missed shifts, not a particular household/farm
- Associated-income estimate intentionally omitted in this version (per Vision Manager)

### Inquiry (Panel 4)
- Removed ambiguous family-of-four + large question-mark treatment
- Placed the **actual candidate inquiry question** as the visual center:
  > What, if anything, does this connection between outdoor working conditions and daily life suggest about the well-being of people you care about?
- Compact Science / AP / PSI separation strip so layers stay distinguishable
- No prescribed answer, guilt, or emotional manipulation
